<h1>How to Get to Vienna and TU Wien</h1>

<p>Vienna is conveniently accessible by both air and rail.</p>

<p>The closest airport is <strong>Vienna International Airport (VIE)</strong>. From the airport, the journey to TU Wien takes approximately 35–45 minutes, depending on the connection.</p>

<p>Vienna's main railway station, <strong>Wien Hauptbahnhof (Wien Hbf)</strong>, is also very well connected to TU Wien. From the main station, the journey to Karlsplatz takes approximately 15 minutes by underground.</p>

<h2>From Wien Hbf to TU Wien</h2>

<p>From <strong>Wien Hbf</strong>, take the <strong>U1 (red line)</strong> in the direction of <strong>Kagran</strong> to <strong>Karlsplatz</strong>. This is two stops. At Karlsplatz, use the exit towards <strong>Karlsplatz</strong> or <strong>Resselpark</strong>. TU Wien is only a short walk from the station.</p>

<h2>From Vienna Airport to TU Wien</h2>

<p>A summary of the different options for travelling from Vienna Airport to the city centre can be found on the <a href="https://www.wien.info/en/travel-info/arrival-departure/airport-to-center" target="_blank" rel="noopener"> official Vienna tourism website </a>.</p>

<p>For current train connections, schedules and tickets, please use the <a href="https://www.oebb.at/en/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">ÖBB website</a>.</p>

<p>We recommend taking the <strong>next available train connection</strong> rather than waiting for a particular service. Tickets can be purchased at the ticket machines at the airport railway station or online.</p>

<h3>ÖBB Railjet to Wien Hbf</h3>

<p>
  The <strong>ÖBB Railjet</strong> provides a direct connection between
  Vienna Airport and <strong>Wien Hbf</strong>.
</p>

<ul>
  <li>Journey time: approximately <strong>15–20 minutes</strong></li>
  <li>Single ticket: approximately <strong>€5.30</strong>, including the Vienna urban area</li>
  <li>From Wien Hbf, take <strong>U1</strong> towards Kagran to <strong>Karlsplatz</strong></li>
</ul>

<p>
  Tickets can be purchased at the red ÖBB ticket machines at the airport
  railway station or online via
  <a href="https://www.oebb.at/en/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">ÖBB</a>.
</p>

<h3>S7 to Wien Mitte</h3>

<p>
  The <strong>S7</strong> connects Vienna Airport with
  <strong>Wien Mitte</strong>.
</p>

<ul>
  <li>Journey time: approximately <strong>25 minutes</strong></li>
  <li>Single ticket: approximately <strong>€5.30</strong>, including the Vienna urban area</li>
  <li>From Wien Mitte, take <strong>U4</strong> towards Hütteldorf to <strong>Karlsplatz</strong></li>
</ul>

<p>
  Tickets can be purchased at the airport railway station or online via
  <a href="https://www.oebb.at/en/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">ÖBB</a>.

<h3>City Airport Train (CAT)</h3>

<p>
  The <strong>City Airport Train (CAT)</strong> provides a non-stop
  connection between Vienna Airport and <strong>Wien Mitte</strong>.
</p>

<ul>
  <li>Journey time: approximately <strong>16 minutes</strong></li>
  <li>From Wien Mitte, take <strong>U4</strong> towards Hütteldorf to <strong>Karlsplatz</strong></li>
  <li>A separate Vienna public-transport ticket is required for the U4 journey</li>
</ul>

<p>
  For current fares, schedules and operating times, please check the
  <a href="https://www.cityairporttrain.com/en/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">official CAT website</a>.
</p>

<h1>Public Transport in Vienna</h1>

<p>Vienna has an extensive public-transport network consisting of underground trains (U-Bahn), trams, buses and suburban trains. Information about public transport, routes and tickets is available from
  <a href="https://www.wienerlinien.at/tickets/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Wiener Linien</a> and
  <a href="https://www.wien.info/en/travel-info/transport/tickets-361360" target="_blank" rel="noopener">
    Vienna's official tourism website
  </a>.
</p>

<p>For 2026, common digital ticket options include:</p>

<ul>
  <li><strong>Single trip:</strong> €3.00</li>
  <li><strong>24-hour digital ticket:</strong> €9.70</li>
  <li><strong>7-day digital ticket:</strong> €25.20</li>
</ul>

<p>Digital tickets can be purchased online. Tickets are also available from Wien Mobil ticket machines and other authorised points of sale. Please check the current fares and ticket conditions before travelling, as prices and ticket options may change.</p>
